# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Esc and the Insert, Home, Page Up, Delete, End and Page Down keys can be blocked too. The strip now has two rows. They are not blocked by default, and they can no longer be used in the global shortcut.
- The numeric keypad can be blocked too, with a single "Numpad" key on the strip. It is not blocked by default, and its keys can no longer be used in the global shortcut.
- Shortcut keys now show the character printed on your keyboard (AZERTY and other layouts), and symbols like * or ^ are easier to read.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Blocks F1 to F12, Print Screen, Scroll Lock and Pause, key by key, even when Pattelock is in the background (Windows, macOS, Linux X11 and Wayland).
- "Lock the whole keyboard" mode, unlocked with Ctrl + Alt + K (Cmd + Option + K on Mac), a click on the cat, or from the tray.
- Customizable global shortcut.
- Counter of paw strikes avoided.
- Animated pixel art cat, 5 coats, 4 accent colors, light, dark and system themes.
- Tray icon in the taskbar or menu bar, and launch at startup.
- Available in English and French, following your system language by default.
- Automatic update check, which can be turned off in the settings.
